Made for a thumb and a spare minute

Everything you love about scrolling a feed, except at the end of it you've actually played something.

👆

Swipe, tap, play

No menus and no loading screens. The next five games are always ready, so the feed feels instant.

✨

A feed that learns

Heart the games you love and the feed brings you more like them. Hit "Meh" and you'll see fewer.

🆕

New games all the time

Games stream in as they're made, so new ones and fixes show up in your feed without an app update.

📳

Uses your whole phone

Tilt to steer, shake to roll, shout to launch, walk to explore, and feel every hit through haptics. Every game also works with touch alone.

🏆

Credits and trophies

Win any game to earn credits, then spend them on boosts and unlocks. Trophies and saves sync with Game Center and Google Play Games.

📱

Built for foldables

Games grow to fill the screen, from small phones to unfolded Galaxy Z Fold and iPhone foldables. Fold it mid-game and keep playing.

Six styles, from dot matrix to next gen

Pick a tab, or let the feed mix them. Every game is an original, inspired by the genres people love on handhelds, consoles and phones.

Casual

Smooth, colourful phone games: one-thumb arcade, puzzles and physics toys.

Pocket

Four shades of green on a chunky pixel screen, like the first handhelds.

Color

The colour handheld era: bright pixels, small screens, big fun.

Advance

Wider screens, detailed sprites, scrolling worlds and racing floors.

Classic Console

Real-time 3D with a cel-shaded cartoon look, inspired by home consoles.

Next Gen

HD 3D with real lighting, shadows, reflections and particles.
