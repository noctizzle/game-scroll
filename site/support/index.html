Help

Something not working, or got a question? Check below, then get in touch.

Contact us

Email support@game-scroll.com. For a broken game, tell us its name (tap the name under the game to see it), your phone model, and what happened. You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads between games. Credits are earned by winning games and can't be bought.

How does the feed pick games?

The "For you" feed mixes styles and learns from you: heart a game to see more like it, tap "Meh" to see fewer. Use the menu at the top to stick to one style, like Pocket or Next Gen.

How do I play a game?

Tap Play. The start screen shows a one-line hint, and tapping the game's name under it shows how to play. Swipe up any time for the next game.

What are credits for?

Win a game (reach its goal score) to earn credits; the first win of each game pays double. Some games sell boosts (used up on your next run) and unlocks (kept forever) for credits.

Will my progress carry over to a new phone?

Yes, if you're signed in to Game Center on iPhone and iPad or Google Play Games on Android. Your credits, wins, trophies and best scores sync through them.

A game wants my location, camera or microphone. Why?

A few games use them as controls, like shouting to launch a rocket. It all stays on your phone and is never recorded or sent. You can say no; every game works with touch too. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an internet connection?

Games download as you scroll, and the app keeps the ones you've played recently, so you can keep playing those offline. New games need a connection.

The app is using a lot of storage.

Open Settings (⚙) and tap Clear cache. Your progress isn't affected.

How do AR games work?

AR · 3D games show 3D objects in your real room through the camera. Hold your phone up and turn on the spot to look around; the objects stay where they are. If you'd rather not use the camera, they play in a virtual room, and you can drag to look around instead of moving your phone.

Does it work on foldables and tablets?

Yes. Games grow to use the bigger screen, and you can fold or unfold in the middle of a game.

Can I make my own game?

Yes! Read the maker guide. If you don't code, you can suggest an idea.
